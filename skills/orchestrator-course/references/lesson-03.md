# Lesson 3 - Your 90 day plan
*Position · about 30 minutes · artefact: `position/pathway.md`*

## Recall
Answer in a line each, out loud or here, before the new material. Retrieval first, teaching second.

1. Which of the four headings on your rules card was hardest to fill in honestly?
2. What did your redaction pass change, and what did it almost miss?
3. Where does your employer line come from: policy, a person, or your own reading?

## What you are making
Three dated commitments: one skill to build, one artefact to finish, one person to show it to.

## Why it matters
A plan with three items gets done. A plan with twelve does not. This lesson is where the course becomes personal: the next nine lessons exist to make your plan real.

## The demo
The coach writes its own 90 day plan on screen: one skill (teach the course without notes), one artefact (the portfolio page for the course), one person (a past student who will test it), each with a date and the evidence that would prove it happened.

## Your turn
Write `position/pathway.md`:
- The skill, in one sentence, with the date you will be able to demonstrate it.
- The artefact, named, with the date it will be finished.
- The person, named, with the date you will show them.
- One line: what you will stop doing to make room.

## The check
1. Three commitments, each with a date inside 90 days.
2. Each commitment names its evidence: a demo, a link, a meeting.
3. The plan does not depend on buying anything expensive.
4. The "stop doing" line exists and is specific.

Failure signs: commitments without dates, or a plan that is really a list of courses to watch.

## Sealed when
`position/pathway.md` passes and the student can say the three commitments from memory.
