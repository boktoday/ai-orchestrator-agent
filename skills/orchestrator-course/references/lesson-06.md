# Lesson 6 - Make it critique itself
*Prompt craft · about 40 minutes · artefact: `prompts/library.md`*

## What you are making
A library of five prompt patterns you will reuse, each with a worked example from your own work.

## Why it matters
One-shot answers are the amateur version of this craft. The professional loop is: define pass criteria first, ask for a draft, ask the model to mark its draft against those criteria, fix the gap, then check it yourself. The loop is the skill.

## The demo
The coach writes a 200 word brief with a rubric of four criteria, generates a draft, asks the model to score its own draft against the rubric, shows the score, then fixes the weakest line. It then checks one factual claim in the draft against a source and shows the model was wrong in a confident sentence.

## Your turn
Fill `prompts/library.md` with five patterns, each with a short template and one real example:
1. The brief.
2. The context pack.
3. The rubric loop.
4. The redaction pass.
5. The verification pass.

## The check
1. Five patterns, each with a template and a real example.
2. The rubric loop includes the criteria written before the draft.
3. The verification pass shows one claim checked and one claim corrected.
4. The student can explain why self-critique is not verification.

Failure signs: five names with no examples, or a rubric written after the answer.

## Sealed when
`prompts/library.md` passes, and the student has said which pattern they will use first at work tomorrow.
