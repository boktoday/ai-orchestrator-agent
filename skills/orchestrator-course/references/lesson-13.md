# Lesson 13 - Verify before you trust
*Guardrails · about 40 minutes · artefact: `guardrails/verification.md`*

## Recall
Answer in a line each, out loud or here, before the new material. Retrieval first, teaching second.

1. Write your schedule in five fields, then in plain English with the timezone.
2. Which run was on time, and which did you start by hand?
3. What makes the job safe to run twice?

## What you are making
A verification log for your own portfolio: every claim, how it was checked, and what the check found.

## Why it matters
Confident output is the default setting. The orchestrator's value is not generating more; it is knowing which sentences survive contact with reality. This lesson turns that from a virtue into a habit with a written trail.

## The demo
The coach takes a claim from its own last piece of work, states how it will be checked, runs the check, and shows one claim that failed. It fixes the page and records both in the log. The lesson is the failure, not the success.

## Your turn
Write `guardrails/verification.md` with a row per claim on your portfolio:
1. The claim, quoted.
2. How you checked it: a command, a click, a source.
3. What the check found.
4. The fix, if any.

You must include at least one claim that failed and what you did about it.

## The check
1. Every claim on the portfolio has a row.
2. Each check is something another person could repeat.
3. At least one failure is recorded honestly.
4. The portfolio now matches the log.

Failure signs: a log of all passes, or checks that say "reviewed by me".

## Sealed when
The log exists, the failed claim was fixed in public, and the student can say which claim was hardest to check.
