# Lesson 9 - Your portfolio
*Build · about 45 minutes · artefact: a live portfolio URL*

## What you are making
One page that shows three pieces of your work, each with a link, a date and a line on what you did.

## Why it matters
People do not read claims; they click links. A portfolio that opens in ten seconds and proves three things beats a CV paragraph every time. This is also the artefact the live Saturday review is built on.

## The demo
The coach builds a portfolio page with three cards, one of them deliberately weak, and rewrites it live: from "AI enthusiast with a passion for innovation" to "A shift-opening checklist used by two cafes, built in an afternoon, edited after a barista broke it". The point is receipts.

## Your turn
Build your page with three cards, from your own work:
1. The mini app from lesson 7.
2. The chore from lesson 8.
3. One earlier piece, or a fourth thing you finish this week.

## The check
1. Every card links to something that loads.
2. Every card says what you did, in first person, without adjectives.
3. Every card has a date.
4. The page works at phone width and names you.

Failure signs: cards that describe rather than link, or claims no link supports.

## Sealed when
The portfolio is live and the three links have been opened by the coach, not just pasted.
