# Lesson 7 - Your first mini app
*Build · about 60 minutes · artefact: a live URL*

## What you are making
One page that does one useful thing for a real person, published at a public address.

## Why it matters
A portfolio of screenshots proves nothing. A link that loads proves you can finish something, and finishing is the skill employers and clients actually pay for. This is the smallest possible complete build.

## The demo
The coach builds a one page tool live: a checklist for opening a shift, a fee calculator, a packing list. It goes from empty file to working page to deployed URL in front of the student, narrating each decision: what it does, who for, the one interaction, why no database, and how it will be checked at phone width.

## Your turn
Build your own page. Rules:
1. One purpose a stranger can understand in five seconds.
2. No login, no database, no payment.
3. It must work on a phone.
4. It must be deployed, not sitting on the laptop.

## The check
The coach opens the live URL and confirms:
1. It loads, with no console errors.
2. It is usable at 390 pixels wide with no sideways scroll.
3. The one interaction completes a real task.
4. The page says who made it and what it is for.

Failure signs: a 404, a page that only exists locally, or an interaction that needs an explanation to find.

## Sealed when
The URL is live, passes the four checks, and is written into `progress.json` as the first portfolio piece.
